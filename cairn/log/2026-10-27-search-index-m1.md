# The search index runs, milestone M1

First implementation of pimdir SEARCH.md (draft-04, untagged, with the grammar, refusals, chunks and coverage rule pimdir logged the same day in 2026-10-27-search-grammar-refusals-chunks). It prepares the 2027 NLnet plan's Task 1 (REPL and MCP): answers typed and schema'd, hits addressable, coverage honest, and a query the grammar does not know refused with a span an agent can correct from.

## What landed

- The `search` feature on `client`, enabled by `cli`; mail-parser 0.11 (`full_encoding`) its only new dependency. migrations/search and queries/search vendored under spec/ and generated by build.rs; tests/spec_drift.rs compares them.
- `client::search`: `PimdirIndex` (open, refresh, search, count, item), the refresh in bounded batches recording the feed's `changed`, the deadline, key reconciliation; `extract` for mail through mail-parser with chunks; `compile` to FTS5 `MATCH` plus SQL.
- `search::query` (the parser, its public AST, `PimdirQueryError`), `search::chunk`, `search::coverage` (`PimdirSearchCoverage`, also filled for the `LIKE` reads by `PimdirReader::search_coverage`).
- CLI `index`, `search`, `count`, `show` with `--json` and JSON Schemas; a refusal exits 1.

## Verification

- `cargo test` default 576, `--features search` 589, `--all-features` 589, all passing; clippy clean on the three, `cargo doc` with broken links denied.
- tests/search.rs over pimdir's 91 search cases: 39 refusals and 50 hit cases answered, 15 skipped as M2 or M3 (each naming a field also checked refused as `unsupported-field`); the 10 chunk cases.
- End to end on a local Stalwart IMAP server synced by neverest into a scratch store, 10,012 mails: RFC 2047 and 2231, Latin-1 quoted-printable, HTML-only base64 and an encrypted message extracted as specified; accents folded both ways; a new mail, a `\Seen` change and an expunge seen by the next one-shot `search` with no explicit `index`. A full rebuild took 0.54 s for an 8 MB index; queries 7 to 74 ms with the process start. That run found bodiless files counted against coverage, so every answer over a store with an attachment stand-in read incomplete: pimdir's coverage rule and this crate now leave files out.

## Not done

- M2 (vCard and iCalendar extraction, occurrences, `horizon_left`, `person:`, `linked:`), M3 (threads, relative dates), M4 (`tag`, `thread`); the consumers.
- `has:attachment` reads `mail_summary.attachment` alone (FTS5 cannot test a column for emptiness), equivalent for mail today. Coverage narrows its scope on top-level `account:`, `collection:`, `kind:` and absolute dates only. The `name` sort orders every hit in memory.
- Measured targets at 50k and 1M items.
